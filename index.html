Authentic Heritage

The Legend of the Golden Cast Iron

Born from a generational passion in Brussels, WAFFLE HOUSE was founded on a simple, uncompromising conviction: a true waffle must be a sensory revelation.

Unlike watery batter used in ordinary pancakes, our authentic Belgian Liege waffles are crafted from a rich brioche dough infused with pure Madagascar bourbon vanilla and genuine Belgian pearl sugar.

When placed onto heavy 180°C cast-iron griddles, the sugar pearls caramelize on the surface, creating an exquisite glassy crunch that encases a warm, pillowy, buttery center.

Henri Van Houten Master Wafflier & Founder

"We don't make fast food. We caramelize moments of pure bliss."

No Shortcuts. Ever.

The Four Pillars of Our Craft

Belgian Pearl Sugar

Imported directly from Wallonia. Does not melt during dough mixing, but bursts into crunchy caramelized crystals on the iron.

48-Hour Brioche Ferment

Slow cold fermentation develops deep butter and brioche notes, producing an incomparably light and tender crumb.

72% Callebaut Dark Cocoa

Velvety chocolate ganache melted freshly throughout the day, providing an authentic bittersweet cocoa punch.

Seasoned 180°C Cast Iron

Traditional heavy grooved iron presses ensure uniform caramelization and iconic deep waffle pockets that capture every drop of syrup.
